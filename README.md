# BNPL
Project Title
Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) Adoption and Household Debt Behaviour Among Gen-Z Consumers in Urban India  
​Project Description

This study investigates the growing trend of Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) fintech services (such as Flipkart Pay Later, Amazon Pay Later, Simpl, and Lazy pay) among Generation Z consumers in urban India. It explores the key drivers behind why young consumers—primarily students and entry-level workers—are rapidly adopting these instant digital credit platforms. The core focus is to evaluate how frictionless micro-lending affects individual spending patterns, encourages impulsive buying, disrupts traditional family and household budgeting habits, and influences the psychological well-being and stress levels of young users.  
​Primary Objective

​To investigate the key determinants of Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) adoption among Gen-Z consumers in urban India and evaluate its subsequent impact on their household debt behavior, budgeting habits, and financial well-being.  
​Specific Secondary Objectives

​Objective 1: Identify Key Adoption Drivers
​To find out the main factors driving Gen-Z consumers to sign up for BNPL platforms, such as app convenience, frictionless digital KYC, targeted social media advertising, and peer influence.  
​Objective 2: Evaluate Impulsive Spending Behavior
​To examine whether the ease of deferring payments encourages unplanned, impulsive purchases on lifestyle goods (fashion, electronics, dining) and leads to post-purchase financial regret.  
​Objective 3: Assess Impact on Household Debt & Family Finances
​To evaluate how personal micro-debts affect overall family budgets, checking whether young consumers rely on parental allowances or family savings to settle outstanding balances.  
​Objective 4: Measure Digital Financial Literacy & Credit Awareness
​To assess the level of awareness among young users regarding hidden costs, penalty structures, late fees, and the long-term impact of payment defaults on their official CIBIL credit scores.  
​Objective 5: Map Psycho-Financial Stress & Anxiety
​To understand the emotional and psychological pressure experienced by Gen-Z consumers when managing multiple fragmented repayment deadlines across different apps simultaneously.
